I am a third year PhD student in the fifth semester of my PhD, working with Dr Amir Rahmati. I work in the fields of Systems/Hardware Security, and my focus area is in building secured systems on existing attacks. 
Before joining Stony Brook University, I was a Master's student at the [George Washington University](https://www.seas.gwu.edu/) in Washington, DC, where I worked with [Dr. Timothy Wood](https://www.seas.gwu.edu/timothy-wood) on Virtual Machine introspection. 
I did my Bachelor's at [Jaypee Institute of Information Technology](http://www.jiit.ac.in/), NOIDA, India.
